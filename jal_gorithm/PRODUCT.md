# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19 + Vite (existing scaffold), `motion` library for animation. Single-page app, no router.

## Users

- The owner: a 4th-year student at Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Pasig focused on web development and modern UI/UX design (React, Next.js, motion-heavy sites), using the site to present their work.
- Visitors: recruiters, professors, classmates, and potential clients evaluating the owner's front-end and design skills.

## Product Purpose

JALGORITHM is the owner's personal portfolio: a single-page, scroll-driven showcase of projects, skills, education, and experience, presented with the modern web animations the owner builds with.

## Positioning

A student portfolio presented under the JALGORITHM name that demonstrates the owner's stated specialty — innovative, motion-rich modern websites — through its own execution.

## Operating Context

Viewed on phones and desktops, often by first-time visitors arriving from links or referrals. Use a consistent light color scheme across devices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page layout; navigation links smooth-scroll to sections.
- Sections, in order: Home, About, Skills/Stack, Projects, Experience/Work, School, Contact.
- Light color scheme only; do not infer a dark theme from device settings.
- Animation via the installed `motion` library: entrance reveals, scroll-triggered motion, hover micro-interactions.
- Mobile responsive across the design-doc breakpoints.
- W3C-valid, semantic HTML.
- Projects, Experience, and School content are template/placeholder entries the owner replaces later.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: JALGORITHM, displayed uppercase.
- Binding visual references supplied by the owner: `design1.md` (Migss-inspired system: black on warm off-white #F5F3F1, Geist Variable type, uppercase rendering, negative letter-spacing on large scale, 0px radii, layered micro-shadows) and `design2.md` (Migss v1 dark system: tomato #ef4444 primary, accent green #22c55e, near-black #0a0a0a background, Poppins, soft radii).
- Owner identity: 4th-year student, Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Pasig; eager about web development, UI/UX design, React/Next.js, and animation/motion.

## Evidence on Hand

- `D:\jalgorithm\design1.md` and `D:\jalgorithm\design2.md`: extracted design tokens from migss.dev (both versions).
- No real project screenshots, headshot, résumé copy, or links yet — placeholders only; never fabricate claims.

## Product Principles

1. Let the artifact lead: the site itself is the strongest proof of the owner's motion and UI/UX skills.
2. Content is replaceable; structure and templates are durable.
3. Both themes must feel designed, not inverted.
4. Respect the two binding design references over any third-party aesthetic.
5. Accessibility and valid markup are non-negotiable alongside motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced-motion support (`prefers-reduced-motion`), keyboard-navigable nav and toggle, visible focus states, WCAG-contrast text in both themes, W3C-valid semantic HTML.
